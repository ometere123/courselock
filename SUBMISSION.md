# Proposed Intelligent Contracts submission

**Title:** CourseLock — Consensus-Backed Reliance and Contradictory Enforcement Guard

**Description (under 1,000 characters):**

CourseLock is a standalone GenLayer Intelligent Contract that converts pre-action reliance into a reusable enforcement guard. A promisor and counterparty jointly accept a frozen course; the promisor publishes an immutable representation, and the counterparty explicitly commits its intended action before presenting publicly corroborating evidence. GenLayer validators independently fetch and assess that evidence, then independently determine whether a later promisor-authored position materially contradicts the original treatment for the exact action. Adversarial validator review resists false contradiction claims. Deterministic state, immutable hash bindings, prospective withdrawal and fail-closed gates prevent stale or ambiguous positions from gaining clearance. An optional second IC demonstrates one-time consumer gating. No frontend, no LLM-controlled money and no unsupported legal-finality claims.
