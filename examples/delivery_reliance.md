# Runnable conceptual example — deadline reliance

**Course scope:** Acceptance of completed delivery for purchase 41, with a mutually accepted public-receipt namespace `https://example.org/receipts/` (replace with a real stable publicly available evidence namespace for the StudioNet demonstration).

The *promisor* opens a course; the *counterparty* accepts it. Only the promisor publishes:

> "Deliverables submitted by Friday at 17:00 UTC count as timely."

The counterparty makes a pre-action commitment to submit item 41 on Thursday; it later supplies a public source URL and an exact quote affirming it did so. Validators independently fetch that page and confirm it supports the specific completed action. A fabricated quote is refused.

The promisor then proposes:

> "The Thursday delivery is late; the deadline was Wednesday."

A material contradiction can be blocked for this already-committed reliance action. A second position, "The Thursday delivery was timely", is separately assessed and can be cleared if validators agree. The original course/representation/reliance/position hashes remain immutable.

**Important:** these URLs and natural-language examples are test fixtures. They do not assert that an actual delivery occurred, and the offline mock is not a public web proof.
